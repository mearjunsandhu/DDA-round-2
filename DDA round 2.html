Our Story

Patiala has a royal past. Maharajas wore the finest fabrics, sharpest turbans and boldest embroidery in the country. Aryaman Saldi grew up around that heritage but never saw it in what people his age wore.

At 17, from his bedroom in 2023, he thrifted jackets, restitched them and sold them to friends. The first drop was 12 pieces and sold out in a day. A tailor uncle in Sheranwala Gate taught him pattern cutting, and a mill owner took a chance on a teenager with a spreadsheet.

Riyasat means a princely state, a kingdom. The idea is simple: old royalty, new rules. Patiala's heritage cut in modern fits, in small batches. Founded by Aryaman, now 20, still in college.
